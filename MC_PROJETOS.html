04.2Normative basis and terminology 0004 · introduction

MC uses the 2026 ISO/DIS 19650-1 and -2 drafts as its internal working protocol. The 2018 editions remain the published standards until replacement; the appointment controls contractual obligations. Draft alignment is not certification.
Working terms: information production requirements (EIR), information production schedule (MIDP/TIDP), information production standard (document 0004), assignment matrix and information production workflow. Existing file IDs and contract titles remain traceable. ISO 7817-1:2024 informs the level of information need; an LOD number alone does not specify it.
