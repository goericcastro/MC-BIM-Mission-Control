MC adopts the publicly verifiable direction of ISO/DIS 19650-1 and -2 as an **internal working protocol** for this project's information management. The drafts are not yet published standards and do not amend the appointment by themselves. ISO records a decision for a new DIS ballot in August 2026; the wording may change. The appointment, applicable law and adopted national standards determine external obligations. Generation is not approval, acceptance or certification. [ISO/DIS 19650-1 status](https://www.iso.org/standard/89703.html).

ISO/DIS 19650-2 is also under development and is intended to replace the published Parts 2:2018 and 3:2020. ISO lists a decision for a new DIS ballot as of August 2026, so its requirements and process details should not be treated as final. [ISO/DIS 19650-2 status](https://www.iso.org/standard/89704.html).

## Process coverage review

The draft overview distinguishes organizational information strategy and AIM work (activities 1-2), project initiation (3), appointment preparation and mobilization (4-7), information production (8), and project completion (9). MC records the project-level evidence for activities 3-9. Organizational AIM governance and formal acceptance remain decisions of the appointed parties; they are not inferred from a completed form. Revisit requirements and appointments when a trigger event or new appointment occurs. [ISO/DIS 19650-1 public preview](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui?_escaped_fragment_=iso%3Astd%3Aiso%3A19650%3A-1%3Adis%3Aed-2%3Av1%3Aen).
